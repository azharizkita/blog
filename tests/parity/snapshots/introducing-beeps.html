Introducing Beeps

Let’s be real—when you’re feeling lonely, and your mind is running wild, sometimes all you need is someone to talk to. But what happens when there’s no one around? That’s exactly where the idea of Beeps comes in.

For me, Beeps is a substitute for X (formerly known as Twitter). In case you’re wondering why I decided to ditch X, you can check out my thoughts here: Life Update.

So, what makes Beeps different? Well, the design is inspired by something familiar—a simple iMessage chat bubble. Why? Because I wanted it to feel personal, like a note to myself or a quick message to share. The philosophy behind Beeps is simple: it’s a place for me to remind myself, to express thoughts, or even just to make myself feel a little less lonely. And who knows? Maybe it’ll work the same way for you too, like receiving a message just for you.

I like to call it a Beep because, well, it’s as straightforward as a Tweet. Nothing complicated, just a quick way to share what’s on your mind.

So, stay tuned for my Beeps!
